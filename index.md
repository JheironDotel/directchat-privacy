# Privacy Policy — Direct Chat No Save

**Last updated: November 25, 2026**

[Español](./es/)

This Privacy Policy explains how Direct Chat No Save, developed by Jheiron Dotel, handles information when you use its Android application.

Direct Chat No Save allows users to initiate conversations through WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business without saving phone numbers to their contacts.

The application does not require an account and does not operate its own servers for storing users' phone numbers, messages or other personal content.

## 1. Information processed by the application

Direct Chat No Save may process information that you voluntarily enter, paste, share or save, including:

- Phone numbers and international dialing codes.
- Message text.
- Conversation history created within the application.
- Favorite entries.
- Message templates.
- Application settings and preferences.
- Images or other content you explicitly select or share through supported features.

The application processes this information to provide its features.

Direct Chat No Save does not require access to your device's contact list and does not read your existing WhatsApp conversations.

## 2. Local data storage

Depending on the features you use, Direct Chat No Save may store phone numbers, message text, history entries, favorites, templates and preferences in the application's private storage on your device.

The application's history represents records of actions performed within Direct Chat No Save. It is not a copy of your WhatsApp conversations.

Direct Chat No Save does not synchronize this information with its own servers.

You can manage or delete saved information using the available application features or by clearing the application's data through Android settings.

## 3. Clipboard and shared content

Direct Chat No Save can process text that you explicitly paste from your clipboard or share from another application.

This information is used to identify relevant phone numbers, prepare messages and provide the requested functionality.

The application does not continuously monitor your clipboard in the background.

## 4. WhatsApp and external applications

When you choose to initiate a conversation, Direct Chat No Save may transfer the selected phone number, prepared message or supported content to WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business or an external browser.

These transfers occur as part of an action initiated by you.

Once the information is transferred, its subsequent processing is governed by the privacy policies and terms of the receiving service.

Direct Chat No Save is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by WhatsApp or Meta Platforms.

For more information, visit:

- [WhatsApp Privacy Policy](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy)
- [Meta Privacy Policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/)

## 5. Advertising

Direct Chat No Save uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads may collect and share technical information associated with the application and advertising services, including:

- IP addresses, which may be used to estimate general geographic location.
- Application and advertising interactions.
- Diagnostic and performance information.
- Device and application identifiers.
- Android Advertising ID and other applicable identifiers.

Google may process this information for advertising, advertising measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention.

The availability and personalization of advertisements may depend on your region, privacy preferences, device settings and applicable requirements.

Direct Chat No Save does not intentionally include phone numbers, message text, favorites or message templates in advertising requests.

For additional information about Google's data processing practices, visit:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Mobile Ads data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/next-gen/privacy/play-data-disclosure)

## 6. Consent and privacy choices

Direct Chat No Save uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage privacy choices associated with advertising.

Where applicable, Google may display privacy messages that allow users to provide, refuse or manage consent and exercise applicable privacy choices.

Direct Chat No Save requests advertisements only when the Google consent SDK indicates that advertising requests are permitted.

Users may access the **Privacy Options** entry within the application when Google UMP determines that this option is required or available.

Through these options, users can review or modify their privacy preferences where applicable.

Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily prevent all advertisements from being displayed.

The main functionality of Direct Chat No Save remains available independently of advertising availability.

## 7. Advertising identifiers

Google Mobile Ads may use the Android Advertising ID when available and permitted.

You can manage or delete your advertising identifier and adjust advertising-related preferences through your Android device settings, depending on your Android version.

Advertising identifiers are subject to Google's privacy practices and applicable device settings.

Additional information is available at:

[Google Ads Settings](https://myadcenter.google.com/)

## 8. Android backup

Certain application preferences or locally stored information may be included in Android's backup or device transfer mechanisms, depending on the application's backup configuration and your device settings.

These mechanisms are managed by Android or the applicable backup provider.

Direct Chat No Save does not operate an independent cloud backup or synchronization service.

You can manage Android backup settings through your device settings.

## 9. Third-party services

Direct Chat No Save uses or interacts with the following third-party services:

- Google AdMob — advertising.
- Google User Messaging Platform — advertising consent and privacy preferences.
- WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business — user-initiated conversations.
- External browsers — opening links when requested.
- Android and applicable system services — application functionality and backup mechanisms.
- GitHub Pages — hosting this Privacy Policy.

These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies.

When you visit this Privacy Policy website, GitHub may process technical information, including your IP address, for hosting, operation and security purposes.

For more information, see the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## 10. Data retention and deletion

Information stored directly by Direct Chat No Save remains on your device according to the application's storage behavior and your actions.

You can remove saved information using the available deletion features, clear application data through Android settings or uninstall the application.

Information previously included in Android backups may remain subject to the applicable backup service's retention and deletion settings.

Direct Chat No Save does not maintain separate copies of your locally stored content on its own servers.

Information processed independently by Google, WhatsApp, GitHub or other external providers is subject to their respective retention policies.

For privacy-related deletion requests or questions, you may contact the developer using the email address provided below.

## 11. Data security

Direct Chat No Save uses Android's application storage mechanisms to protect locally stored information from ordinary unauthorized access by other applications.

Integrated third-party services use their own security mechanisms. Google Mobile Ads uses encrypted connections for its data transmissions as described in Google's documentation.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.
